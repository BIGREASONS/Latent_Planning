\section{Results}

\subsection{Architecture Expressiveness vs. Semantic Preservation}

\textbf{Observation:} Under the evaluated configurations, the Transformer transition architecture reduced the Oracle Gap relative to the MLP and Linear baselines, despite all three architectures achieving comparable geometric reconstruction (MSE and cosine similarity scores).

\textbf{Interpretation:} This suggests that transition architecture expressiveness influences semantic preservation, and that geometric metrics alone are insufficient to distinguish between them.
